> **"twelve refuted predictions from the manuals, and one profile that located

**This repo quotes "ten manual-derived predictions were measured here and ten
were refuted."** The fork's own closing assessment says twelve.

> tally disagreeing with itself was not sloppy transcription of a stable figure —

## And rpcsx already attributes the failures to the pipe column

**I formed that hypothesis independently this morning, from the `BCAX` forward
prediction and the `TBX2` retro-prediction. It is stated in its own ledger:**

> **"`TBL` is `2 2 V` on A715 — full width, unlike the `V0`/`V1` traps that sank
> most manual-derived ideas."**

> **The fork that coined the prior had already diagnosed its own failures as
> pipe-column traps.** This repo quoted the headline — *do not pick a lever from a
> manual* — **and not the diagnosis sitting beside it.**

**That is the fifth instance this session of a fork's own document carrying the
qualification its headline lacks**, and it is the pattern this repo already names:
**start at a fork's routing table, and read a document's own corrections before
relying on a section.**

## And it says the manual work was NOT wasted

> **"The manual-derived work was not wasted — it confirmed the lowerings, closed
> `sse2neon`, and found the empty `mov_rdata` branch. But the ratio is stark."**

**Three concrete products**, one of which — the `sse2neon` closure — **this repo

**That is a materially different claim from "do not pick a lever from a manual",
and it is the one worth carrying.**
